Eight games, 600 levels

Every one is a whole game, played in chapters of ten levels.

  • Magnet Zing!

    Drop your magnets inside the rope without letting them snap together. Ten arenas, against one to three friendly robots, or with friends on one phone.

    100 levels

  • Swap Bazar New

    A market twist on match-3. Swap treats, collect what each stall needs and clear the jelly. Every drop is fixed, so a clever plan always pays off.

    70 levels

  • Traffic Escape

    Clear the parking jam. Tap a car and it drives the way it faces; if something blocks it, it bumps and rolls back.

    70 levels

  • Path Escape

    Untangle a lawn of friendly snakes. Each one slides out head first along its own body, so choose the order with care.

    70 levels

  • Queue Escape

    Board every passenger in color order on buses, trains, ferries, planes and rockets. Fill every spot and you are boxed in, so think ahead.

    70 levels

  • Sliding Puzzle

    Slide the tiles back into order on a walnut board, and rebuild 30 pictures. Boards grow from 3x3 to 6x6.

    70 levels

  • River Crossing

    Load the boat, choose who rows, and never leave the tiger alone with the goat, across 18 painted places.

    80 levels

  • Tower Trails

    A Tower of Hanoi journey through seven worlds, ending in the Pirate Coves, where a fourth tower opens new routes.

    70 levels

Safe by design

Built for players of every age, and for the grown-ups who hand over the phone.

  • No ads, no lives, no purchasesNothing to buy, and nothing interrupting play.
  • Nothing collectedNo accounts, no sign-up, nothing to type. Everything stays on the device.
  • Plays offlineNo Wi-Fi or internet needed, anywhere, at any time.
  • Six players, one phoneEach with a funny name, a matching animal face, and their own progress.
  • Never stuckHints whenever you need one, and picture lessons for every new idea.
  • Quiet when you need itMusic, sound effects and vibration each switch off.
