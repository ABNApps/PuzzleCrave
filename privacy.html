Privacy Policy

PuzzleCrave

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PuzzleCrave app ("the app") handles information. PuzzleCrave is published on Google Play by Gaddam Vikram (ABN Apps).

The short version

PuzzleCrave does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. Everything the app remembers stays on your phone or tablet.

What the app keeps on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the app saves these things on your device only:

  • Player names and icons. Each player picks a funny name from built-in word lists (for example "Captain Wobbly Penguin") and an animal icon. Nobody types a name, and names are never sent anywhere.
  • Game progress. Levels solved, stars, best times and moves, puzzles in progress, and daily challenge results for each player on the device.
  • Settings. Music, sound effect, and vibration choices, and the sky each player picks for the home screen.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, birthdates, photos, contacts, or location.
  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not use the advertising ID or any other device identifier.
  • It shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, chat, or social features.
  • It contains no analytics or advertising tools.
  • It does not share information with anyone, because it does not collect any.

Children

PuzzleCrave is made for players aged 5 and up, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, no information is collected from children. We designed it to follow the Google Play Families policies, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

Crash reports

If you have chosen, in your Android settings, to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Android and Google Play may send Google anonymous crash and performance information about apps on your device. That sharing is controlled by you and by Google, not by PuzzleCrave. We may see anonymous, grouped crash statistics in the Google Play Console to fix problems. They do not identify you.

How long information is kept, and how to delete it

Information saved on your device stays there until you remove it. To delete all of it, uninstall the app, or clear the app's storage in your Android settings (Settings > Apps > PuzzleCrave > Storage > Clear storage).

Security

Because the app keeps information only on your device and sends nothing over the internet, your information is protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if a future version adds online leaderboards, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the "Last updated" date above.
