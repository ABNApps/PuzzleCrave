Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Email abnapps2026@gmail.com and tell us what happened, which puzzle you were on, and which phone or tablet you are using. We read every message.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Stars, best times, and puzzles in progress are kept for each player on the phone, and never leave it. Installing an update keeps everything. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it, and it cannot be recovered, because there is no copy anywhere else.

Can several people play on one phone?

Yes, up to six. Each player picks a funny name from built-in word lists and an animal icon, and keeps their own progress in every game. Add or switch players from the Players button on the home screen.

How do I turn off the sound?

Open Settings from the menu. Music, sound effects, and vibration each have their own switch, and there is a choice of sky for the home screen.

I am stuck on a puzzle

Every puzzle has a Hint button that shows one safe next step, and undo is unlimited and free. Using a hint means a level scores at most two stars, so you can always come back later for the third.

Does the app need the internet?

No. PuzzleCrave plays completely offline and does not connect to the internet at all.
