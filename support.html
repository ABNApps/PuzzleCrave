Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Each player's stars, best moves and times, puzzles in progress, and gems are kept on the phone, and never leave it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes them, and they cannot be recovered, because there is no copy anywhere else.

Will updating to 2.0.0 keep my progress?

Yes. Your levels, stars and gems are kept. Daily puzzles have retired, so their results and calendar are not carried over, and the home screen now has one fixed sky.

Can several people play on one phone?

Yes, up to six. Each player builds a funny name from a few built-in words (nobody types), gets the matching animal face, and keeps their own progress in every game. Tap Players at the bottom of the home screen, or your animal face, to add or switch players.

How do I turn off the sound?

Open Settings from the bottom of the home screen, or from the Settings button in any game. Background music, sound effects, and vibration each have their own switch, and Gentle effects means less shaking and fewer sparkles.

I am stuck on a puzzle

Tap How to Play on any puzzle for a picture guide to the rules. Each new idea also gets a short picture lesson the first time it appears.

The Hint button shows a next move, but a level won with a hint scores at most two stars, so you can come back later for the third. Undo is free in most games, but undone moves still count toward your stars. In Tower Trails, undo works in warm-ups only, and in Magnet Zing! in Practice only.

What are gems for?

Earn a gem by winning three neighboring levels in a chapter with three stars each, without a Golden Hint. Spend a gem on a Golden Hint: it shows the next three moves and still lets you win all three stars.

Can I play sideways?

No. Since version 2.0.0 the app plays upright (portrait) on phones and tablets.

Does the app need the internet?

No. PuzzleCrave plays completely offline and does not connect to the internet at all.
